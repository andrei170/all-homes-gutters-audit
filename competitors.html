The Row That Matters, And How We Found It
A pixel can hide inside a tag manager — so we opened theirs

Gutter Helmet's pages show a Google Tag Manager container, GTM-K8HC9F9, and no pixel in the page source. That is exactly how a Meta pixel usually looks from the outside: invisible.

So we fetched the container itself. Inside it: six references to fbq, connect.facebook.net, and a Google Ads conversion tag, AW-16481619161.

They are tracking conversions and building retargeting audiences. You are running Facebook ads and doing neither.

That is the whole competitive gap in one row. Not content — you are ahead on content. Not towns — you have 48 of them. Measurement.

Where You Are Genuinely Ahead
This is a weak field on everything except instrumentation
  • 82 pages and 48 service areas. Neither independent rival publishes a readable sitemap, and neither appears to run town pages at the scale you do.
  • You publish your pricing. Almost no contractor in any trade does this. It filters out the people who were never going to buy, before they reach your phone — which matters when you are doing the admin yourself from the road.
  • You publish financing. On a $10,000 job that is often the difference between a yes and a think-about-it.
  • FAQPage, OfferCatalog and AggregateRating markup. That is the structured data that gets you into Google's answer boxes and puts stars next to your listing.
  • A named About page for you personally. In a trade full of anonymous LLCs, being a findable human is worth more than most marketing.
And the one thing the franchise has that you do not

Gutter Helmet is a national brand with national budget, and it is fully instrumented. Every dollar they spend teaches their advertising something. Every dollar you spend on Facebook currently teaches yours nothing.

You cannot out-spend them. You can out-local them — 48 towns, published prices, a real name and a review habit will beat a franchise in Ashtabula County. But not while their ads compound and yours reset.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • The Meta Ad Library refused every request we made today — HTTP 403 directly and a CAPTCHA notice through a proxy, across four different routes. So nothing here describes, counts or assesses any advertisement by any firm, including yours. That is a closed door on our side, not a finding about anyone.
  • Big & Small Seamless Gutters blocked us outright with a 403. We have recorded nothing about them and that absence means nothing.
  • Ohio Gutter Company's real page count, because their sitemap URL returned an error page.
  • What Gutter Helmet actually spends, bids on, or earns. We can see the tags are installed. We cannot see budgets, keywords or results, and we make no claim about them.
  • Everyone's Google Business Profile, review count and map position, including yours.
  • Anyone's revenue, crew size, pricing, close rate or job volume, including yours.
  • Every gutter company in Northeast Ohio. This is the set we could find and verify, not a census.

You have built more than anyone here. You are measuring less than the one company that matters.

A pixel is an afternoon. The 82 pages took months, and you already did that part.
